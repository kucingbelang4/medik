A common painkiller used to treat aches and pain. It can also be used to reduce a high temperature (fever). It's available combined with other ingredients in some cold and flu remedies.

Co-codamol (Paracetamol/Codeine)

A mixture of two different painkillers: paracetamol and codeine. It's used to treat aches and pains including headaches, muscular pain, and toothache when everyday painkillers haven't worked.
